## What this does to the storyboard

- Clips 01–16 are represented by the exterior, directory, corridor, elevator and Room 523 material.
- `waitingroom.mp4` is an insert between the directory and corridor. It violates the storyboard’s “nearly empty” lobby instruction, but it strengthens the bureaucratic scale and gives the protagonist a believable intermediate space.
- Clips 17–19 are not individually covered by the available renders. The two `authentic-late-1950s` Room 523 videos are the closest substitutes, but they should be treated as alternate coverage, not exact dialogue continuity.
- `room523.last.stuck.mp4` covers the tiny-door processing sequence.
- The pneumatic trilogy gives the escape a physical mechanism instead of cutting directly from the alarm to the exterior.
- The two apartment videos work as a post-administration return-home coda: the bureaucracy has followed him into domestic space.
- The stop-motion clip works as an aftershock or nightmare contamination before the final exterior.
- `master-continuity-lock-3.mp4` is best reserved for the final exterior because it ends in black and can carry the final fade/title treatment.
