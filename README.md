# ADMINISTRATION — Flux.3 reconstruction archive

This repository contains the source Flux.3 renders, rejected/alternate renders, storyboard notes, and prompt versions used to reconstruct the film *ADMINISTRATION*.

The videos are stored with Git LFS. The source renders are not claimed to be frame-continuous: several are alternate generations and should be selected by endpoint and composition before assembly.

## Contents

- `prompts/original-and-reference-prompts.md` — original continuity, storyboard and sequence prompt material reconstructed from the project records.
- `prompts/rejected-and-diagnostic-prompts.md` — rejected prompt variants and the continuity problems they produced.
- `prompts/corrected-20-second-cue-sheet.md` — corrected 20-second Flux.3 cue sheet, including the officials walking away, entering the 20 cm door one at a time, countdown, red flashes, escape, two-second silence and metallic klonk.
- `storyboard/proposed-kafka-district-17-order.md` — available-render ordering notes.
- `videos/source/` — source and current scene renders.
- `videos/rejected/` — explicitly rejected or diagnostic renders.

## Intended regeneration structure

The completed film is divided into connected 20-second units. Use the final frame of each generated unit as the start-image anchor for the next unit. Keep one protagonist, one document case, one building and one office geometry throughout.

## Important continuity rules

The four officials always keep their backs toward the protagonist. They rise facing away from him, walk approximately 20 metres to the far-right wall, and crawl through the same physically real 20 cm square door one at a time. The protagonist remains seated until the distorted female Slavic countdown begins. He then exits, closes the office door, holds for exactly two seconds of silence, and the sequence ends with one loud metallic `KLONK`.
