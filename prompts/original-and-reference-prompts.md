# Original and reference prompt material

## Master visual language

Authentic late-1950s Eastern European black-and-white 35 mm motion picture, 4:3 Academy ratio, 24 fps, high-contrast panchromatic film, dense organic grain, faint emulsion scratches, dust, occasional vertical scratch, subtle gate weave, slight frame flicker, crushed blacks, pale concrete highlights, soft halation around lamps, deep focus, slow deliberate camera movement, realistic human motion, solemn ominous bureaucratic surrealism. Sparse mono optical soundtrack with low hiss, distant ventilation, concrete reverberation and hard echoes. No modern objects, modern signage, digital displays, contemporary clothing, smartphone look, glossy CGI, color grading, subtitles or captions except the final title card.

## Protagonist lock

The same tall, lean Eastern European man, approximately 38, angular pale face, pronounced cheekbones, tired deep-set eyes, short dark hair combed carefully back, clean-shaven. Worn charcoal wool overcoat over a dark 1950s suit, white shirt, narrow black tie, polished black shoes, one battered rectangular black leather document case. He is polite, apprehensive and physically controlled; fear appears in his eyes, shallow breathing and sweat rather than exaggerated gestures.

## Storyboard basis

Title card and brutalist exterior; approach and entrance; directory; repeating corridors; waiting room; Room 523; descent toward level −50; black office; four faceless officials; tiny door; countdown and escape; pneumatic transport; apartment coda; final rain exterior.

## Prompting method

Use 20-second clips with a supplied start image. Preserve the exact final composition of the preceding clip as the next clip’s first frame. Describe one major action per clip, lock the camera axis, and explicitly prohibit unintended movement, face reveals, duplicated characters, changing door scale and lighting/exposure drift.
